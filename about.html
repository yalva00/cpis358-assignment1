About Us

Sunny & Land Beauty Salon is a warm and welcoming place where beauty meets comfort. We started with a simple idea: everyone deserves to feel confident and cared for in a calm, natural space.

Our team offers haircuts, hair coloring, styling, and makeup using quality products and a personal touch. With our website, you can explore our services, check our prices, and book your appointment in a few clicks.
